🔄 How Undo Works

1. A patient is called from a queue.

2. The operation is pushed into the Undo Stack.

3. The patient is added to the Recently Treated Stack.

4. When Undo is clicked, the latest operation is popped.

5. The patient is returned to the appropriate queue.
